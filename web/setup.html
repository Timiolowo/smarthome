How to find your Phone's IP address:
  • iPhone: Open Settings > Wi-Fi → tap the (i) icon next to your home Wi-Fi → copy IP Address (e.g. 192.168.18.3).
  • Android: Open Settings > Wi-Fi / Network → tap your connected Wi-Fi → copy IP Address.
  • Tip: You can also use your phone's hostname like iPhone.local instead of numbers.
